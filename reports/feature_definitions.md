## 1. Overview & Analytical Framework

This document specifies the ten engineered customer-level features developed in Role B for the Retail IQ intelligence platform. The feature space compresses 3,748 transaction-level records across 200 distinct customers into a tabular profile where each row corresponds to exactly one unique customer (`customer_id`).

### Analytical Assumptions on Currency & Campaign Calendars:
1. **Currency Units:** As documented in `data/data_dictionary.md`, transaction amounts and discounts are recorded as numeric values without an explicitly specified fiat currency (neither USD `$` nor INR `₹`). All monetary features (`monetary`, `avg_order_value`) are expressed strictly in standardized **monetary / currency units**.
2. **Promotional Responsiveness vs. Causal Lift:** No external campaign calendar exists in the repository. The feature `promo_lift` measures the observed difference in Average Order Value (AOV) between discounted transactions (`is_discounted == 1`) and non-discounted transactions (`is_discounted == 0`). This metric reflects observed purchasing behavior under discounts, **not** causal campaign effectiveness or incremental uplift.

The features span six core customer behavioral dimensions:
1. **RFM Dimensions:** Recency, Frequency, Monetary, and Average Order Value.
2. **Discount Sensitivity:** Offer Dependency and Average Discount Percentage.
3. **Customer Loyalty:** Six-Month Repeat Purchase Rate.
4. **Merchandise Engagement:** Category Diversity.
5. **Temporal Dynamics:** Trimester Spending Trend.
6. **Promotional Responsiveness:** Promotional Lift.

## 3. Detailed Mathematical Definitions & Methodology

### A. RFM Dimensions

#### 1. `recency_days`
* **Definition:** The number of elapsed calendar days between the customer's most recent transaction date and the fixed reference snapshot date.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{recency\_days} = \text{Date}(\text{Snapshot}) - \max(\text{Date}(\text{transaction}_i))$$
* **Parameters:** Fixed snapshot date is **October 30, 2026** (`2026-10-30`). The latest transaction recorded in the cleaned dataset is October 29, 2026.
* **Domain Interpretation:** Lower values indicate active, recently engaged customers; higher values indicate churn risk or dormancy.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 1 day, Max = 163 days, Median = 40 days.

#### 2. `frequency`
* **Definition:** Total count of distinct orders placed by the customer across the six-month observation window.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{frequency} = \text{CountDistinct}(\text{order\_id})$$
* **Order-Level Rule:** In `transactions_clean.csv`, each row represents one distinct order. Grouping by `customer_id` and taking `nunique(order_id)` guards against accidental line-item duplication.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 3 orders, Max = 45 orders, Median = 18 orders.

#### 3. `monetary`
* **Definition:** Total cumulative spend generated by the customer, calculated as the sum of final paid amounts after discounts across all transactions.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{monetary} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{amount}_i$$
* **Reconciliation Rule:** Total dataset monetary value ($\sum \text{monetary} = 4,137,071.75\text{ units}$) exactly reconciles with source transaction amounts ($\sum \text{amount} = 4,137,071.75\text{ units}$).
* **Observed Range:** Min = 902.82 units, Max = 75,582.94 units, Median = 14,283.50 units.

#### 4. `avg_order_value` (AOV)
* **Definition:** Average net spend per unique order in currency units.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{avg\_order\_value} = \frac{\text{monetary}}{\text{frequency}}$$
* **Domain Interpretation:** Distinguishes high-ticket bulk/luxury buyers from frequent micro-purchasers.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 225.51 units, Max = 2,043.68 units, Median = 875.20 units.

---

### B. Discount Features

#### 5. `offer_dependency`
* **Definition:** Proportion of a customer's total orders that involved an applied promotional discount (`is_discounted == 1`).
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{offer\_dependency} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{order\_id} \text{ where } \text{is\_discounted} = 1)}{\text{frequency}}$$
* **Boundaries:** Strictly bounded within $[0.0, 1.0]$.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 0.0000 (20 customers never use discounts), Max = 1.0000 (3 customers only buy on promotion), Median = 0.2967.

#### 6. `avg_discount_pct`
* **Definition:** The arithmetic mean of discount percentages applied across all unique orders placed by the customer.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{avg\_discount\_pct} = \frac{1}{\text{frequency}} \sum_{i=1}^{\text{frequency}} \text{discount\_percent}_i$$
* **Calculation Level:** Computed at the order level. Full-price orders contribute 0.0% to this average.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 0.00%, Max = 35.28%, Median = 4.51%.

---

### C. Customer Loyalty

#### 7. `repeat_rate`
* **Definition:** The proportion of repeat purchases relative to total purchase events across the observation window.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{repeat\_rate} = \frac{\text{frequency} - 1}{\text{frequency}}$$
* **Edge Case Handling:**
  - For $\text{frequency} = 1$: $\text{repeat\_rate} = 0.0$ (no repeat orders).
  - For $\text{frequency} \le 0$: Undefined ($\text{NaN}$).
  - In our dataset, all 200 customers have $\text{frequency} \ge 3$, so repeat rate is strictly positive for all customers.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 0.6667 (customer with 3 orders), Max = 0.9778 (customer with 45 orders), Median = 0.9444.

---

### D. Category Diversity

#### 8. `category_diversity`
* **Definition:** The count of unique merchandise product categories purchased by the customer.
* **Available Categories:** `Home & Kitchen`, `Electronics`, `Grocery`, `Beauty`, `Sports`, `Clothing`.
* **Treatment of `Unknown`:**
  - Role A filled missing category values in raw transactions with `Unknown` (1 row in the entire dataset: customer `C0200`).
  - **Decision:** In our primary pipeline, `Unknown` is **excluded** from diversity calculations (`exclude_unknown=True`) because `Unknown` represents an unclassified missing-data artifact, not an authentic merchandise category.
  - With `Unknown` excluded: `C0200` has 3 distinct categories (`Grocery`, `Home & Kitchen`, `Electronics`). If included, `C0200` would have 4 categories. For the other 199 customers, the count is identical.
* **Observed Range:** Min = 2 categories, Max = 6 categories, Median = 6 categories. Over 62% of customers have purchased across all 6 product categories.

---

### E. Spending Trend

#### 9. `spend_trend`
* **Definition:** Percentage change in customer spending between the first half (Trimester 1) and second half (Trimester 2) of the observation window.
* **Window Specifications:**
  - **Trimester 1 ($P_1$):** May 1, 2026 – July 31, 2026.
  - **Trimester 2 ($P_2$):** August 1, 2026 – October 31, 2026.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{spend\_trend} = \left( \frac{\text{Spend}_{P2} - \text{Spend}_{P1}}{\text{Spend}_{P1}} \right) \times 100$$
* **Treatment of Zero Denominators:**
  - Customers with zero spending in Trimester 1 ($\text{Spend}_{P1} = 0$) cannot have a mathematically valid percentage change.
  - **Governance Rule:** Zero denominators are strictly returned as **$\text{NaN}$ (undefined)**, never artificially imputed as 0% or $\pm \infty$.
  - Customer `C0082` onboarded on August 6, 2026 ($P_1 = 0.00\text{ units}, P_2 = 2,080.96\text{ units}$). `spend_trend` is correctly marked as $\text{NaN}$.
  - Customers who stopped purchasing in Trimester 2 have $\text{Spend}_{P2} = 0$, resulting in a valid trend of $-100.0\%$.
* **Observed Range:** Min = -100.0%, Max = +164.13%, Median = -39.93% (among the 199 defined customers).

---

### F. Promotional Response

#### 10. `promo_lift`
* **Definition:** The relative percentage difference in Average Order Value (AOV) when purchasing under promotional discount versus full price.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{promo\_lift} = \left( \frac{\text{AOV}_{\text{discounted}} - \text{AOV}_{\text{non-discounted}}}{\text{AOV}_{\text{non-discounted}}} \right) \times 100$$
* **Baseline & Denominator:**
  - **Denominator:** $\text{AOV}_{\text{non-discounted}}$ represents baseline, unprompted customer spend per order.
  - **Numerator:** $\text{AOV}_{\text{discounted}} - \text{AOV}_{\text{non-discounted}}$ represents the incremental currency units per order.
* **Promotional Exposure vs. Observed Discounts:**
  - The dataset contains recorded discount flags (`is_discounted`). Specific campaign codes (`MONSOON26`, `DIWALI26`) are populated for only 135 orders out of 1,101 discounted orders, and no promotional calendar table exists.
  - We do not fabricate synthetic campaign dates. Lift is calculated directly on observed promotional versus non-promotional purchase behaviors.
* **Handling of Missing Comparison Groups:**
  - If a customer has **0 discounted orders** (20 customers, e.g. `C0002`), $\text{AOV}_{\text{discounted}}$ is undefined.
  - If a customer has **0 non-discounted orders** (3 customers: `C0121`, `C0147`, `C0191`), $\text{AOV}_{\text{non-discounted}}$ is undefined.
  - In both cases, $\text{promo\_lift}$ is strictly recorded as **$\text{NaN}$ (undefined)**. Exactly 23 customers have undefined promo lift.
* **Observed Range:** Min = -75.05%, Max = +167.08%, Median = -24.07% (among the 177 defined customers).

## 4. Governance & Coordination Guidelines for Role C

### Exclusion of `persona_true`
* `persona_true` is synthetic ground-truth metadata provided in the raw dataset.
* It is **strictly excluded** from `customer_features.csv`.
* Machine learning algorithms (clustering, PCA) trained by Role C must not ingest `persona_true` to prevent label leakage.

### Missing Value Treatment Options for Role C
When preparing `customer_features.csv` for distance-based clustering (e.g. K-Means) or dimensionality reduction (PCA):
1. **`spend_trend` (1 missing value, `C0082`):**
   - Role C may consider imputing with median trend ($-39.93\%$) or introducing a missing indicator flag `is_new_customer = 1`.
2. **`promo_lift` (23 missing values):**
   - For customers with `offer_dependency == 0` (20 customers), lift is absent because they never participate in sales. Role C may impute with 0% or cohort median with an `unexposed_to_promo = 1` binary flag.
   - For customers with `offer_dependency == 1` (3 customers), impute with cohort median with a `promo_only_buyer = 1` binary flag.
3. **Feature Scaling:**
   - Standardize features using `StandardScaler` or `RobustScaler` due to skewness in `monetary` and `frequency`.
